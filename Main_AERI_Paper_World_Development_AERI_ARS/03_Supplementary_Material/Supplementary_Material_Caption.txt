Supplementary Material for "Measuring Economic Risk in Africa: The African Economic Risk Index and African Risk Score." Contains detailed pillar definitions, the formal PCA conversion, the PVAR specification and inference, expanded exact-text deduplication results, additional external-identification results, all-country AERI/ARS outputs and monthly country AERI paths, together with material moved from the paper to meet the length limit: an extended literature discussion, the keyword taxonomy and worked scoring example, the eight-pillar PCA weights, additional descriptive exhibits, the data-collection architecture, the archive-depth diagnostic and the external-identification diagnostics.
