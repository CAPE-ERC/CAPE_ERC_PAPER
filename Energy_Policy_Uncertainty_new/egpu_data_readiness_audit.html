Executive summary

Proceed with a qualified, balanced-sample analysis—not a full-panel claim. The long and wide EgPU files agree exactly and the country-month keys are unique. However, the EgPU grid has seven missing cells, 1,266 source article files are reported as skipped, and macro coverage is incomplete and uneven. A complete quarterly EgPU–GDP-growth–CPI candidate exists for 18 countries over 45 quarters; the broader 36-country overlap is unbalanced.

The audit did not fit a PVAR. Lag selection, unit-root and residual tests, GMM validity, stability, IRFs, and FEVDs therefore remain not run. No coefficient, causal interpretation, or statistical significance is reported here.

4. Recommended next steps

  1. Prespecify the 18-country balanced sample or justify an estimator for the 36-country unbalanced overlap; publish the exact country crosswalk.
  2. Resolve and document the seven missing EgPU country-months, review zero-index months, and assess outliers where only one site contributes.
  3. Investigate skipped article files and the 272 zero-denominator site-months. Rebuild the index only if access to its external raw article archive is available.
  4. Confirm GDP growth and CPI units, vintage, and actual/forecast status. State the quarterly EgPU and CPI aggregation rules.
  5. Then estimate the specified PVAR with constrained instrument sets and report lag selection, stationarity/dependence checks, AR(2), Hansen/Sargan, stability, and country-bootstrap IRF/FEVD uncertainty.

Scope and reproducibility note

This audit validates the CSV outputs and macro workbooks packaged in the public repository. It does not rescan the source news archive; the pipeline references an external article directory that is not present here. The run manifest records an index window ending 2026-09. Results can change if the underlying inputs or index are updated.

For machine-readable test names, results, thresholds, and notes, use the companion audit CSV.
