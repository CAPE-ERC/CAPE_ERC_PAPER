# News-Based Economic Risk and GDP Forecast Evaluation — Version 2

This package contains the revised five-section manuscript, 12-point main text, separate figure/table notes and numbered text footnotes, supporting information, editable LaTeX sources, the integrated research flowchart, and the original replication files.

1. **01_Main_Manuscript** — PDF, editable LaTeX, figures (including `Figure_Research_Framework.tex` and `.pdf`), and tables.
2. **02_Supporting_Information** — PDF, editable LaTeX, additional derivations, scoring rules, diagnostics, and tables.
3. **03_Title_Page** — standalone title-page PDF and LaTeX.
4. **04_Replication_Data_and_Code** — original code and derived inputs, unchanged.
5. **05_Editorial_Notes** — original methodological audit and the Version 2 change record.

The abstract and introduction now identify two related elements: an independently constructed monthly newspaper-based GRE risk measure and an evaluation of its forecasting contribution when GDP histories are temporally disaggregated from annual data. Proposition 1 and all reported statistical estimates have been retained, and the limitations concerning final-vintage training labels remain explicit.

The PDF documents and tables were compiled from the accompanying LaTeX sources. Footnotes under tables and figures are formatted separately from numbered prose footnotes.
